# Typing basics

## Spell it: letter, then haraka

Type each letter, then its vowel. Qalam adds sukūn and shadda for you.

| You type | You get | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `kataba` | كَتَبَ | letter + haraka, three times |
| `maktab` | مَكْتَب | `k` has no vowel after it, so it gets sukūn (Smart sukūn) |
| `darrasa` | دَرَّسَ | a doubled letter gets shadda |
| `kitaAbuN` | كِتَابٌ | `A` is alif, `uN` is tanwīn ḍamm |
| `saAala` | سَأَلَ | `A` followed by a haraka is a hamza; Qalam picks the seat |
| `alshamsu` | الشَّمْسُ | the article knows sun and moon letters |
| `madrasat'uN` | مَدْرَسَةٌ | `t'` is tā' marbūṭa |
| `ktb` | كتب | no vowels typed: bare letters |

The full list of keys is the **[key chart](../LAYOUT.md)**. The short version:

| Type | Gets | Type | Gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a i u` | ـَ ـِ ـُ | `aN iN uN` | ـً ـٍ ـٌ |
| `A` | ا (alif) | `A` + haraka | hamza (seat automatic) |
| `'` | hamza with no haraka | `e` | ع |
| `t'` | ة | `Y` | ى |
| `AA` | آ | doubled letter | shadda |
| `th dh kh sh` | ث ذ خ ش | `g` / `gh` | غ |
| `H S D T Z` | ح ص ض ط ظ | `z` / `zh` | ز |
| `aA` = `aa` | long ā | `iy` = `ii`, `uw` = `uu` | long ī, ū |
| `o` | sukūn by hand | `^` | dagger alif (ـٰ) |

Capitals are the "heavy" letters, and **only Shift** makes a capital, so Caps Lock can't turn س into ص by accident.

## While you type a word

- The word shows **underlined** in place (Mac) or in a **small box** near the cursor (Windows).
- **Space**, **Enter** or punctuation puts the word in.
- **Backspace** removes the last *English* key, and the Arabic updates.
- **Esc** gives back the English letters, so you can type an English word without switching keyboards.

## Options while typing

Under the word, a panel shows up to **4 versions**:

```
 qaraA
 1  قَرَا     as typed
 2  قَرَأ     alif + hamza
 3  قَرَأَ     hamza + fatḥa
 4  قرا      no harakat
```

- **↑ / ↓** choose a version, **Space** puts it in. You can also click one.
- Row 1 is always exactly what you typed.
- Qalam **remembers** the version you picked for each word, and whether you usually pick the version without harakat. This stays on your computer only. Forget it in [Settings](settings.md).
- The panel's **Sukūn** and **Harakat** buttons switch those settings on the spot.

Don't want the panel? Switch off **Show options while typing** in [Settings](settings.md).

## Switching between Arabic and English

- **Mac:** **🌐 Globe** or **Control-Space** switches keyboards. The menu bar shows ق when Qalam is on.
- **Windows:** **Ctrl + Alt + A** or a click on the ق tray icon. The icon is green when Arabic is on.

## Qur'an style

Choose **Qur'an (Uthmani)** in [Settings](settings.md) to get alif waṣla (ٱ), dagger alif, maddah, silent-letter marks, waqf signs and āyah numbers. The [key chart](../LAYOUT.md) lists the extra keys.
