# First-time setup

Right after you install it, Qalam walks you through a short setup, one step at a time. Every choice is saved as you make it, you can go **Back** or jump to any step, and **Skip setup** is always there. Everything in it can be changed later in [Settings](settings.md).

- **Mac:** the setup is in the Qalam app window.
- **Windows:** the setup opens in your browser. It's a page served by Qalam itself on your PC (nothing goes online). Open it again any time: right-click the **ق** tray icon → **Setup & settings…**

## The steps

| # | Step | What you choose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Welcome** | Nothing. Shows how spelling turns into Arabic. On Windows you can already try typing in a box. |
| 2 | **Keyboard** (Mac) / **On & off** (Windows) | Mac: **Add Qalam now** puts Qalam in the input menu, and **Switch to Qalam now** selects it. Windows: shows the on/off shortcut (Ctrl + Alt + A) and the tray icon. |
| 3 | **Style** | **Everyday** (standard spelling) or **Qur'an (Uthmani)**: ٱ, dagger alif, maddah and mushaf spelling. Optionally the Madinah-mushaf sukūn (قۡ). |
| 4 | **Harakat & sukūn** | Harakat: **Full**, **Only what I type**, or **None**. Sukūn: **Smart** (only at a stop inside a word), **Full** (every stop) or **Off**. Each choice shows a preview. |
| 5 | **While typing** | **Show options while typing** (the panel of up to 4 versions), **Smart spelling** (اللَّه، هَٰذَا), **Arabic digits** (١٢٣). |
| 6 | **Shortcuts** | Change the shortcuts if they clash with ones you use. See [Shortcuts](shortcuts.md). |
| 7 | **Fonts** | Mac: the fonts the app shows Arabic in. Windows: where to get good Arabic fonts for your other apps. See [Fonts](fonts.md). |
| 8 | **Updates** (Mac) / **Windows** | Automatic update checks and installs. On Windows also **Start with Windows**. |
| 9 | **Practice** | Start the optional **20-word practice** (about 5 minutes, covers every special key), or finish. |

Not sure what to pick? The defaults (Everyday, Full harakat, Smart sukūn, options on) suit most people.

## Run the setup again

- **Mac:** Qalam → **Settings** → **Keyboard status** → **Run setup again**.
- **Windows:** right-click **ق** → **Setup & settings…** → **Walk through the steps**.

## The 20-word practice

Each word teaches one key you'll need: a long vowel, a hamza, ة, the article, tanwīn… The practice shows exactly what to type. You can **skip a word** or **skip the practice** whenever you like, and come back to it later:

- **Mac:** the **Practice** page in the sidebar.
- **Windows:** right-click **ق** → **Letters, words & practice…**
