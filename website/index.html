Why Qalam

Built for students of Arabic and the Qur'an who know the letters but not the Arabic keyboard layout.

مَكْتَب

Spell it, get it

Letter, then haraka. Sukūn and shadda appear by themselves. No layout to memorise.

ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ

Qur'an style

Alif waṣla, dagger alif, maddah, waqf signs and āyah numbers, as in the mushaf.

أ إ ؤ ئ

Hamza done right

Type A or ': Qalam picks the seat. Options while typing let you choose another.

كتب

Harakat your way

Full, only what you type, or none at all. Switch with a shortcut while you type.

⌨︎

Every app

Word, Pages, Chrome, WhatsApp, VS Code… It's a real keyboard, not a website.

١٢٣

Private and offline

No internet, no account, no tracking. It only checks GitHub for updates.
